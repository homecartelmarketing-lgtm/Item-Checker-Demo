General rules:
- Suppliers often put several variants (round, oval, square, wall sconce, floor lamp) in one gallery. Only the variant in Image 1 is valid.
- A wall sconce, table lamp or floor lamp is never a valid photo for a ceiling light, even if the style matches.
- A styled room photo of the exact same fixture is valid.
- A close-up of crystals, glass, socket or hardware is valid only if nothing in it contradicts Image 1.
- A photo with several different fixtures is unsure unless Image 1's fixture is clearly the main subject.
