# Pendant Light Quality Control Inspection Rules

Pendant lights range from minimalist single-drop pendants to multi-light linear clusters.

## 1. Head Count & Multi-Drop Configuration
- If Image 1 is a **Single-Head Pendant Light** and Image 2 shows a **3-light or 5-light cluster**, mark as **MISMATCH**.
- Multi-head canopy models represent distinct Shopify variants with different pricing.

## 2. Shade Geometry & Diffuser Style
- Cylindrical shades vs Globe/Sphere glass shades vs Cone shades:
  - If shade geometry is fundamentally different, mark as **MISMATCH**.
  - Smoked glass vs Amber glass vs Clear glass: Any color tint variation is a **MISMATCH**.

## 3. Acceptable Side Photos
- Lifestyle shot showing the single pendant hung above a bedside table, kitchen island, or dining nook.
- Close-up of the glass shade texture, brass socket, or braided cord.
- Dimension specification drawing with matching SKU measurements.
