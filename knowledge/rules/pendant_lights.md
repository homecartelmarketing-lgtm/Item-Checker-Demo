Pendant rules:
- Single-head vs multi-head (3-light, 5-light cluster) is a different variant.
- Different shade geometry (cylinder vs globe vs cone) is a different variant.
- Clearly different glass tint (clear vs smoked vs amber) is a different variant, if the lighting makes it unambiguous.
- Lifestyle photos above an island or bedside, and close-ups of shade, socket or cord, are valid if consistent with Image 1.
