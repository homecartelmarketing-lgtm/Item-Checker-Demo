Chandelier rules:
- Rectangular/linear and round/oval chandeliers are always different variants. Shared crystal style or gold finish does not change that.
- Long rectangular chandeliers usually hang from two or more cords; round ones usually from one central rod. A different cord count is a different variant.
- Single-tier vs multi-tier (2-tier, 3-tier) is a different variant.
- Same design in a clearly different length (for example 3 arms vs 5 arms, 8 heads vs 12 heads) is a different variant.
